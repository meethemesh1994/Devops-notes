SLI -> the acutal measured number based on which we can say application is as per our standards or not. latency and availability(app should be available 99% of the time)
SLO -> service level objective. internal agreement with in our team. 99.5% of the requests should be in 300ms
SLA -> promise to our client, failing this we need to pay penalities. for example 99% availability and latency is 500ms

1000 requests
995 requests -> success
5 requests -> allowed failure
0.5% error budget

990 requests -> success
10 requests -> failure

fraction(out of 1) and percentage(for 100)

failure -> 1

budget consumed = 1/0.5 = 2 = 200%
we consumed 2X more than our budget

SLI = 990/100 = 99
SLO = 995/1000 = 99.5
